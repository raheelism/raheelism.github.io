1. Introduction and Architectural Commitment

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which Muhammad Raheel Anwar (independent software engineer and developer) collects, processes, and protects user information across the mobile application Taqwa: Islamic Prayer Times, Quran & Azan (package: com.raheelism.taqwa) and related personal mobile products published on the Google Play Store.

Taqwa is engineered with a strict offline-first, zero-telemetry architecture. We hold that religious observations, prayer times, and personal study habits must remain private to the individual. The application does not require user accounts, emails, or personal profiles to operate.

2. Location Permissions and Processing

Strict local on-device processing guarantee

Taqwa computes solar prayer angles and Qibla directional bearing exclusively in your device processor volatile memory. Your geographical coordinates are never uploaded to any remote server, cloud datastore, or advertising partner.

The application may request access to device location services (access_fine_location and access_coarse_location) strictly to enable two features:

  • Astronomical Prayer Calculations: Calculating the five daily prayer times (Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) requires latitude, longitude, and elevation using standard astronomical equations (such as Muslim World League, Umm Al-Qura Makkah, ISNA, Egyptian Authority, and Karachi conventions).
  • Sensory Qibla Compass: Computing the direct great-circle azimuth bearing from your present geographic position to the Holy Kaaba in Makkah (21.4225° N, 39.8262° E).

Background Location: If you choose to enable automatic prayer schedule recalculations while traveling between cities, Taqwa accesses location in the background solely to refresh local prayer alerts on your phone. This background location is evaluated locally, never stored historically, and never transmitted outside your physical device.

Manual City Selection Alternative: You can choose to look up your city manually from the built-in offline world database, which requires zero location permissions.

3. Timely Notifications and Exact Alarms

Under Google Play policies governing exact alarm and notification scheduling, Taqwa uses system alarm mechanisms (post_notifications, schedule_exact_alarm, and use_exact_alarm) solely for time-critical spiritual notifications:

  • Triggering local Azan audio calls to prayer at the exact calculated minute for each of the five daily Salah.
  • Delivering user-configured pre-prayer preparation reminders and morning or evening Athkar notices.

All alarms and notifications are scheduled locally on the Android operating system. No remote push notification servers or device identification tokens are used.

4. Quran Recitation and Foreground Media Service

To provide listening experiences for the Holy Quran, Taqwa requests foreground media permissions (foreground_service and foreground_service_media_playback):

  • To play Quranic recitations continuously while the screen is locked or while switching between apps, with standard playback controls in the notification drawer.
  • To cache Surah audio tracks and translation files locally on your device storage for uninterrupted offline playback.

The application has zero access to personal files, photos, media libraries, contacts, or documents stored on your phone.

5. Zero Third-Party Advertising and Analytics

Taqwa is committed to clean utility:

  • No Ad Networks: The app contains no third-party advertising SDKs, banner trackers, or behavioral profiling components.
  • No Telemetry SDKs: We do not bundle third-party analytics suites that harvest device identifiers or track behavioral patterns.
  • No Data Monetization: We never sell, rent, license, or share user data with data brokers or marketing intermediaries.

6. Google Play Data Safety and User Deletion

In compliance with the Google Play Data Safety declaration:

  • Data Collected: Zero personal identification data collected or uploaded.
  • Data Shared: Zero user data shared with third parties.
  • Data Storage and Retention: All user configurations (such as prayer convention, bookmarks, downloaded Surahs, and Athkar counters) reside strictly in your local device SQLite storage.
  • Immediate Data Deletion: You can completely erase all data at any moment by navigating to Android Settings > Apps > Taqwa > Storage > Clear Storage, or by uninstalling the app.

7. Children’s Privacy Protections

Our mobile applications do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from children under the age of 13, in strict adherence to the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child safety standards. The application is completely suitable and safe for family educational use.

8. International Rights and Regulatory Compliance

Users residing in the European Economic Area (under the General Data Protection Regulation / GDPR) and California (under the CCPA and CPRA) hold specific rights regarding personal data. Because Taqwa does not collect, transmit, or process personal data on remote infrastructure, your privacy rights of access, objection, restriction, and complete erasure are guaranteed intrinsically by design on your own hardware.

9. Policy Modifications

We may periodically revise this policy to reflect application enhancements or regulatory evolutions. The most current version will always be published at this public URL with an updated revision date.
